The problem

You have a leave policy. But do you have a leave process?

Having a policy in place doesn’t guarantee a positive employee experience.

Extended employee leaves are often managed through disconnected spreadsheets, email exchanges, outdated documentation and informal conversations. HR teams are stretched thin, managers are left to navigate sensitive situations without adequate training, and employees are expected to piece together information during some of life’s most significant transitions.

The result?
  • Inconsistent employee experiences across departments.
  • Unnecessary administrative work for HR.
  • Confusion around benefits, compensation and responsibilities.
  • Managers who are unsure how to support their employees.
  • Disruptive returns to work.
  • Increased employee disengagement and retention risks.

Leave management shouldn’t depend on who your manager happens to be.

Our solution

From better processes to better outcomes.

Whether you’re looking to improve your existing practices or outsource the coordination of employee leaves, Manela meets your organization where it is.

Manager Training

Give your managers the confidence to get it right.

Managers play a critical role in shaping the employee leave experience. Yet many have never received formal training on how to navigate extended absences or support employees returning to work.

Manela equips your leaders with practical tools and the interpersonal skills needed to manage these transitions effectively and compassionately.

Training topics can include:
  • Responding to an employee’s leave announcement.
  • Navigating sensitive and unexpected leaves.
  • Managing workload redistribution and team transitions.
  • Establishing appropriate communication boundaries.
  • Supporting employees without creating pressure to work.
  • Preparing for an employee’s return.
  • Conducting effective reintegration conversations.
  • Recognizing and reducing unconscious bias.
  • Supporting long-term employee engagement and retention.
What you receiveBetter-equipped managers, more consistent leadership practices and a more supportive workplace culture.
